10 password security rules

  • 1. Be unique. One password per account limits credential stuffing.
  • 2. Go long. Prefer 16+ characters or a passphrase.
  • 3. Avoid personal facts. Names and birth years are guessed first.
  • 4. Skip common passwords. Lists of them are public.
  • 5. Never reuse. One breach should not open every door.
  • 6. Use a manager. It creates and stores random passwords.
  • 7. Turn on MFA. A stolen password alone is not enough.
  • 8. Never share. No legitimate support asks for your password.
  • 9. Beware phishing. Check the address before typing.
  • 10. Change after compromise. Rotate on suspicion, not on a calendar.

Plaintext vs hash

Password→+ random salt→slow hash (Argon2id, bcrypt, scrypt, PBKDF2)→stored hash

Hashing is one-way verification; encryption is reversible. Fast hashes alone make offline guessing cheap. The salt makes identical passwords store differently. See backend/services/hashing_demo.py (synthetic demo only).

Entropy and its limits

Entropy ≈ L × log2(N), where L is length and N the character pool. It assumes random choice. Password123! looks diverse yet is highly predictable, so this tool combines entropy with pattern, common-password and context checks. Guess-resistance labels are educational estimates only.
